PHOTOS FOR THE WEBSITE
======================
Put real photos here (JPG, landscape, about 1600 px wide, under 400 KB each).
Which photo each service uses is set in src/data/photos.js (change a path there to use your own).

  images/hero.jpg                  Big picture at the top of the Home page (your best gate or job)

  images/services/                 One photo per category (items/ holds one per service):
    iron.jpg        MS / iron gate
    wrought.jpg     Wrought iron / designer gate
    steel.jpg       Steel structure or shed
    aluwin.jpg      Aluminium windows
    aludoor.jpg     Aluminium or glass doors
    glass.jpg       Glass partition / glass doors
    shower.jpg      Shower cabin
    shutter.jpg     Rolling / shop shutter
    grills.jpg      Window or balcony grill
    railing.jpg     Railing or staircase (SS, glass or iron)
    allsteel.jpg    Steel racks / shelving
    fiber.jpg       Fiber glass roof / shed

  images/projects/                 Your own finished jobs. Link each one in
                                   src/data/content.js -> PROJECTS, e.g.
                                   image: "/images/projects/main-gate.jpg"

Use photos of your own work, or stock photos you have the right to use
(e.g. from unsplash.com or pexels.com, which are free for business use).
